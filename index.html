How To Use

Use this page as a turn order helper for Aeon's End. Pick player count first, then draw and manage cards with the icon actions. Changing player count starts a fresh game by rebuilding the draw pile and emptying discard.

  • Players Select 1-4 players and reset to a new game setup for that count.
  • 🔊/🔇 Toggle sounds on/off.
  • ? Open this help popup.
  • Draw Pile Click on pile to draw next card.
  • ⇅ Reveal next card and choose to place it on top or bottom of the drawpile.
  • 👁 Peek top card without moving it.
  • ★ Reveal next two cards and order them in chosen order.
  • ⚡ Reveal entire draw pile and reorder it.
  • Discard Pile Click to open discard history (newest at top).
  • ☠ Return all Nemesis cards from discard to draw pile and shuffle.
  • ← Return one player card from discard to draw pile and shuffle.
  • City / Nemesis +/- Adjust health counters for the city or the nemesis.
  • ☀️ Keep the screen awake while this app is visible (where supported).

Players

Add 1-4 players and optionally rename them. Applying changes starts a new game and resets draw/discard.
